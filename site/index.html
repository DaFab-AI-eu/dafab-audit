Tonight

Every number on this page is recomputed from the public API each night. Items are validated against the shipped schemas, their declared assets are matched with the files attached in the catalogue and with replica states in storage, and a rotating share plus every changed item is downloaded and checked byte by byte against the recorded checksum.

Scopes, collections and versions

Inventoried and verified counts include results carried over from earlier nights for items whose document has not changed since. A version is the processing version recorded in the document, or the id suffix for items without one. Assets whose files live outside the catalogue, such as the full Copernicus product links kept on a Sentinel-2 item, are counted as external and not verified. Surplus files are attached to an item in the catalogue but declared by no asset with a catalogue link, so nobody can reach them; they do not make an item incomplete, they cost storage.
